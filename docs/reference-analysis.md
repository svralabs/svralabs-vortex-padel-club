## 2. Structural Breakdown

1. **Header:** Minimalist brand centered with search bar and booking CTA on right, section anchors on left.
2. **Hero:** Asymmetric composition. Left 55% dedicated to impactful typography, social badge, and sport center tag. Right 45% features high-resolution padel racket and neon ball flatlay on blue turf texture.
3. **About Section:** Two-column split with paragraph copy and dark pill CTA on the left; staggered two-card photographic showcase on the right.
4. **Experience Section:** Contrast interplay — a deep dark court card on the left featuring night play, paired with an editorial story card on the right featuring gear and amenities.
5. **Facilities Carousel:** 4 distinct venue cards (Court, Lounge/Cafe, Gym, Tournaments) with category pills and navigation arrows.
6. **Member Stories:** Social proof featuring an authentic female padel athlete portrait, prominent quote typography, and carousel controls.
7. **Action Banner:** Panoramic blue court card with dynamic athletic lunge shot and group membership invitation.
